Discarding training points lowers the cost of the regression and loses accuracy, and the heuristics used to choose which points to keep usually leave that loss unquantified. This section makes it explicit \cite{bib:kriging-subsampling}, by rewriting the discrepancy between the full and the subsampled estimators as a function of the information carried by the discarded data alone.
